HOW TO ADD / REPLACE PHOTOS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Save your picture as a standard JPEG (e.g. "ghee.jpg").
Step 2: Copy or drag & drop it into this folder:

Step 3: Refresh your browser at http://localhost:5000 (Ctrl + F5).
        Your new image will appear instantly on the website!
